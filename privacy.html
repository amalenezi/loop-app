Loop privacy

Last updated 11 November 2026

Loop is a canvas for reviewing live screens and leaving comments that a coding agent acts on. This page explains what Loop stores when someone shares a canvas with you.

What we store

  • Your Google profile basics — name, email address and profile photo — when you sign in with Google. We use them to check that the canvas was shared with your address and to show who left each comment.
  • Your comments, the element you pointed at, and any marks you drew.
  • The canvas itself — the screens the owner chose to share.

What we don't do

  • We don't access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • We don't sell or share your data, and there's no advertising or tracking.

Who can see it

Only the canvas owner and the people they invited can open a canvas and its comments. Data is stored with Supabase (database and file storage) and the site is served by GitHub Pages.

Removing your data

You can delete your own comments on the canvas. To remove your account or anything else, contact the person who invited you, and they'll ask the Loop owner to delete it.
